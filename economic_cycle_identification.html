The indicator dashboard — track these weekly
ISM Manufacturing PMI — above 50 = expansion, below = contraction. The direction of change matters as much as the level. Crosses above/below 50 are phase transition signals. ismworld.org monthly
Leading
ISM Services PMI — services is 70%+ of US economy. Services PMI diverging from manufacturing = important signal. Services falling below 50 = serious trouble. ismworld.org monthly
Leading
Unemployment rate + NFP — unemployment is a lagging indicator. It peaks AFTER recession starts. Don't wait for it to rise to call a recession — use leading indicators. Watch NFP trend, not single print. BLS monthly
Lagging
Initial jobless claims — weekly. Much faster than monthly unemployment. Rising trend over 4 weeks = early labor deterioration signal. Most responsive employment indicator. FRED: ICSA weekly
Leading
Conference Board LEI (Leading Economic Index) — composite of 10 leading indicators. Three consecutive monthly declines = recession signal with high historical accuracy. conference-board.org
Best composite
Retail sales (real) — consumer spending is 68% of GDP. Month-over-month trend matters. Two consecutive negative months = demand deteriorating. Census Bureau monthly
Coincident
Industrial production + capacity utilization — measures factory output. Late-cycle indicator. High utilization = inflationary pressure. Falling utilization = demand drop. FRED: INDPRO
Coincident
Housing starts + permits — housing leads the cycle by 6–12 months. It dies first and recovers first. Permits are the leading version — they collapse before starts do. Census Bureau monthly
Leading
Credit card delinquencies + auto loan defaults — early warning for consumer stress before it shows in unemployment. Rising delinquencies = Phase 3 → 4 transition approaching. NY Fed quarterly
Leading
Corporate earnings revisions — when analysts revise EPS estimates down across sectors broadly, it's a cycle signal. Negative revisions accelerating = late cycle or recession entry. FactSet weekly
Leading

How to use this to trade SPX and gold right now
1Score each indicator above as early / mid / late / recession. Whichever phase gets the most votes = your current regime. Majority rules — don't need unanimity.
2The phase tells you your directional bias for weeks to months. It does not give you intraday signals — it tells you which direction to lean on every trade until the next indicator update.
3Transitions matter more than the phase itself. When 3+ indicators flip simultaneously from one phase to the next — that is your biggest trade signal. Phase transitions = regime changes = the fat-pitch setups.
4For SPX: mid cycle = long bias all year. Late cycle = reduce, rotate to defensives. Recession = short or flat. Early = aggressive long, buy the laggards.
5For gold: late cycle = accumulate. Recession entry = full long. Mid cycle with falling real yields = still hold. Early cycle = reduce as risk-on resumes and HY spreads compress.
6The trickiest phase is the transition from late to recession — everything looks bad for SPX but gold hasn't moved yet. That lag is your entry window for gold before the real move starts.
